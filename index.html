PRELUDE.

Predicting Recurrence in Early LUng aDEnocarcinoma

Predicting stage I lung adenocarcinoma relapse is no longer a technology problem. It is a coordination problem. PRELUDE is the community pooling whole-slide histology and omics across institutions to fix it.

We are still flying blind in stage I lung adenocarcinoma: up to one-third relapse, and staging alone does not tell us who.

Our pilot, PATH-ORACLE (medRxiv, 2026), trained a foundation-model predictor on 272 stage IA/IB cases from TCGA-LUAD and validated across CPTAC, TRACERx, and KORANYI. Multimodality, histology fused with transcriptomics, beat either alone.

The tech is here. The data is fragmented.

Foundation pathology models have arrived. Transcriptomic profiling is routine. Our pilot showed the math fuses cleanly on 272 stage I cases. The biomarker works.

What's missing is scale. The world's stage IA and IB lung adenocarcinoma WSIs and matched omics sit in institutional silos of 50, 100, 200 cases. No single cohort, however carefully curated, reaches the levels required for clinical translation. Pooled, they do.

PRELUDE is a community effort. Federated by design, contributor-built, contributor-credited. Your data stays with your institution. Your name goes on the paper. Joined, we move the needle. Alone, we don't.

[03]

What we are asking for.

From each joining site, four things:

→ 01
WSIs
H&E of the resected primary, scanned at 20× or 40×.
→ 02
Omics
Bulk transcriptomics preferred. WES, methylation, proteomics welcomed.
→ 03
Outcomes
Recurrence status with date, plus core clinicopathologic variables.
→ 04
A lead
A pathologist, oncologist, or PI willing to co-author and steer protocol.
